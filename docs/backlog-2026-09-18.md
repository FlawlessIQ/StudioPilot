### F4 — When a job stops, so does its crew — SHIPPED (2026-09-19)
Bigger than the archive case it was found through: `crm/commands.ts` owns both
`transitionProject` and `archiveProject` and **never touched
`crewAssignments`**, so *calling off a wedding* left every offer standing too —
including an accepted one belonging to a second shooter holding the date, told
nothing.

Now `features/crew/job-stopped.ts` (mirrored into functions):

- **Cancelling** withdraws every live assignment. Somebody who never accepted
  is withdrawn quietly — the offer vanishing from their portal is the whole
  message. Somebody who **accepted** is withdrawn *and* emailed
  (`crew_assignment_cancelled`), carrying the studio's reason, which
  `transitionProject` already requires for a called-off job.
- **Postponing** drops the offers nobody answered and **keeps** an accepted
  one: the date is moving, not gone, and re-agreeing it is a conversation
  rather than a side effect.
- **Archiving refuses** while anyone is waiting — the same rule archiving a
  *client* already follows. Archiving stays allowed at any stage otherwise,
  which is what it is for; it just cannot hide a job with a live offer on it.
  The refusal names cancelling as the move that actually ends them.
- A cascade still working down its list is closed, so it cannot offer the next
  name on a job that has stopped.

**Sent, not drafted** — a change from the recommendation. Crew mail in this
product is transactional and already auto-sends (the invitation, the reminder),
there is no crew draft-and-approve surface (`communicationDrafts` is
contact-shaped), and the harm of silence falls on a third party who has turned
other work down. Say so if you would rather it waited for approval.

**Latent bug fixed on the way:** the crew-profile archive guard tested
`settled = ["declined", "withdrawn", "completed", "closed"]`. `withdrawn` and
`closed` are not in `assignmentStatusSchema`; `expired`, `cancelled` and
`reassigned`, which are, were missing. So the three commonest ways an offer
ends counted as open and a collaborator whose assignments had all expired could
never be archived. Your production tenant is full of expired assignments.

### B3 — A deployment fault was being written onto studios' connections (2026-09-21)

### H5 — The inquiry form is slow and unresponsive (Gabe, testing as a couple)

Investigation: `inquiry-form-performance-investigation-2026-09-28.md`.

**Measured on prod (`/inquiry`):**

- ~1.5 MB of JavaScript and ~684 KB of CSS (5,918 rules) for a single form.
- Time to first byte ~1.06 s.

**Likely causes, ranked:**

1. **Typing before the page is ready gets erased.** On hydration,
   react-hook-form writes the `""` defaults into the inputs, so whatever was
   typed is lost. 1.5 MB of JavaScript makes that window seconds long on a
   phone.
2. **The venue box re-renders the whole form and runs the full Zod schema
   twice per keystroke.** Also a **bug:** `placeCity()` on typed text
   overwrites the City field on every comma.
3. **Address suggestions are slow.** Each one waits on up to 2 tenant lookups,
   a rate-limit transaction and then Google, one after another. After 120
   requests an hour per IP + browser, suggestions stop silently.
4. **All the app's CSS and JavaScript ship to this public page**, including
   Firebase, the service worker and a body-wide MutationObserver.
5. **The slug lookup runs twice per page load**, uncached. **App Check
   starts only when Send is pressed**, so the submit can wait up to 10 s.

**Next steps:**

- Ask Gabe which link, phone and browser he used, and whether he opened it
  from an app.
- Profile with a phone-class CPU: interactive time and INP per field.
- Get places route latency from the production logs.
- Then apply the fixes and add guard tests:
  - the bundle budget;
  - a test that text typed before hydration survives.

**Target:** interactive in under 2 s on a mid-range phone, keystrokes under
100 ms, suggestions around 400 ms, and nothing typed is ever lost.
