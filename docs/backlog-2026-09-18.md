## E — The demo film

### E1 — FIXED · the false package claim is gone
`~/Documents/studiocue/studiocue-demo.mp4` (1:39, delivered) says at 0:27:

> "A new enquiry lands. StudioCue has already read it, **matched a package to
> what they asked for**, and drafted the reply."

Reading it and drafting the reply are true and automatic. **Matching a package
is not** — there is no `packageId` anywhere in the intake schema. That happens
in `runConsultationAnalysis`, queued when the studio saves consultation notes
(`functions/src/booking/commands.ts:439`).

The fix is two lines instead of one, and the second is a stronger claim than the
original:

> "A new enquiry lands. Before you have opened it, StudioCue has read it, pulled
> out what is missing, and drafted the reply."
>
> "After the call, you save your notes. It recommends the package that actually
> fits what they asked for — and drafts the proposal from their own words."

**Corrected without rebuilding the demo environment** (the machine had ~1 GB
free, which is what OOM-killed the emulator in the first place). Rather than
shoot the consultation beat that the split would have needed, the line now
claims the *date check* — which is real, automatic, and arguably better:

> "A new enquiry lands. Before you have opened it, StudioCue has read it,
> **checked the date against your calendar**, and drafted the reply."

The existing footage supports it, so only one beat of narration was
regenerated. Caption updated to "Read. Checked. Drafted." Still 1:39.

The package-recommendation claim is now **unused**, and is worth adding as its
own beat when the environment is next up — over the booking autopilot, whose
own hero copy says it "recommends an existing package, and prepares a proposal
without inventing pricing or sending anything."

`demo-film/pipeline/layout.mjs` now derives the whole edit — cut list, caption
times, audio offsets — from the measured mp3 durations, and refuses a cut that
runs past the footage it belongs to. It caught exactly that on the first
attempt at this fix.
