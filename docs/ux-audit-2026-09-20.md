# UI/UX audit — 2026-09-20

Walked as a studio owner against a local emulator with the demo seed, driven
through a browser rather than read. Ten task walks; nine findings that a real
studio would hit, one retraction, one unresolved.

**The pattern in almost all of it:** a capability ships into the *engine* and
the *editor*, and the **list screens keep the pre-change shape**. Coverage roles
shipped into the package editor, the proposal and the cascade — the catalogue
still shows `480` and `Yes`. Crew trades shipped into the ranker and all three
forms — the directory still shows a count of specialties. Insurance became a
studio setting — the directory still reports it missing on every row. The lists
are where a studio checks its own work, and they are the last thing updated.

Severity: **P0** loses a wedding or blocks the job · **P1** real confusion,
costs time · **P2** polish.

**All 14 fixed** (execution plan phases 1–3, 2026-09-20). Two things stayed
open on purpose and are recorded at the end: naming the crew in the journey
rail, and an authenticated end-to-end path for the one unresolved observation.

## P1 — worth fixing before the next studio sees it

### 1. The catalogue cannot tell a video package from a photo package
`/studio/packages`. Created "Cinematic Collection" (0 photographers, 2
videographers). It renders **identically** to "The Signature Collection" (2
photographers, 0 videographers):

> Cinematic Collection · Base price $7,200 · Deposit 30% of total · Coverage **480** · **Yes**
> The Signature Collection · Base price $6,500 · Deposit 30% of total · Coverage **480** · **Yes**

- `Coverage` is `includedCoverageMinutes` (`live-domain-view.tsx:134`) — a raw
  `480` with no unit, and never the crew.
- The green pill is the `active` boolean through `live-domain-view.tsx:504`
  (`value ? "Yes" : "No"`): an answer with the question missing.

This is the same failure as the six mis-recorded packages found on 2026-09-19.
For a video-led studio the whole catalogue is indistinguishable from itself.

**Fix:** `describeCoverage(resolveCoverage(record))` already exists and returns
"2 videographers". Render `8 hrs · 2 videographers`; label the status pill.

### 2. The crew directory never shows the trade, and reports insurance nobody asked for
`/studio/crew`. Row facts are `Specialties: 2` (a **count** — which ones?),
`W-9`, `Insurance`.

- The trade shipped on 2026-09-19 is **invisible on the one screen whose job is
  "who do I work with"**.
- `Insurance` renders unconditionally (`live-domain-view.tsx:240`) although G5
  made it a studio setting defaulting to **off**. Every collaborator reads
  "Insurance missing" for a document the studio has said it does not want.

### 3. Cue shows the studio raw exception text
`/studio/copilot` rendered **"Cue could not answer / fetch failed"**.

1. `vertex-token.ts` codes the non-ok response but a **throw** from `fetch` —
   timeout, DNS, network blip — escapes uncoded as `TypeError: fetch failed`.
2. `copilot.ts:2105` returns `caught.message` verbatim.
3. `friendlyError` anticipates this exact class —
   `/failed to fetch|network ?error|load failed/i` — but that is the *browser's*
   wording. Node's undici says **"fetch failed"**, reversed, so it slips past
   `looksHumanWritten` and renders as-is.

In production this is any Vertex timeout or quota exhaustion.
`tests/error-copy-coverage.test.ts` cannot catch it: it scans
`new Error("CODE")` and this is a raw provider throw.

### 4. Cue cannot be exercised anywhere but production
`cloudAccessToken()` reads `metadata.google.internal`, which exists only on GCP,
and `PROVIDER_MOCK_MODE` does not cover the copilot tool loop. **No local walk,
no staging walk and no test can drive a Cue turn end to end.**

That is a structural reason Cue's problems reached the reference studio first.
The four causes fixed in the G batch were all found by reading a production
transcript after the fact — and none of them needed a real model to find. The
turn *shape* (subject matching, flow placement, the compact job card,
done-ness) is deterministic and should be walkable offline.

### 5. Mobile: the delete control sits above the primary action
`/studio/projects/{id}` at 375px. Order down the page:

1. Title and stage · 2. Tabs · 3. Date, venue, lead ·
4. **Archive job** · 5. **⚠ Delete this job permanently** ·
6. *Your next move — "Did this go ahead?"*

The most destructive control in the product is the second thing on a phone,
above the job's actual next action. **Introduced 2026-09-19**: the delete
control joined `interruptionEl`, which on mobile renders third, before the
thread. On desktop the same fragment is in the rail and lands at 1906px.

### 6. "Who is on this job?" is answered at 87% of the page
Desktop, 1440x900, 3163px document: header 149 · thread+rail 582 · lifecycle
lanes 1974 · **Your crew 2768** · More detail 2991.

The panel shipped for G4 reads well — "Jordan Reid · Second photographer ·
Accepted". But Gabe's question was *"I can't see anywhere where my staff is for
this job"*, and the answer is below the thread, the prepared tray and the
lanes. The journey rail already says **"Crew confirmed · All 1 offered role
accepted"** at 700px and names nobody — that is where this belongs.

### 7. An 818px column of white beside the rail
`.job-thread` is 556px and `.job-rail` is 1374px in a two-column grid. On any
job without a long thread — every new job, every imported one — over half the
screen below the fold is empty while the rail runs on alone.

## P2 — polish

8. **People tab icons are squashed to nothing.** Measured SVG widths in the
   Clients/Crew/Team/Vendors tab bar: **2px, 13px, 11px, 0px** against a 14px
   height. Clients renders as a dot, Vendors as nothing. Missing
   `flex-shrink: 0`.
9. **Trade checkboxes stack under their labels.** `.crew-form-preview form label`
   (0-1-2) beats `.crew-trade-options label` (0-1-1), so `display:grid` wins
   over `inline-flex`. Introduced 2026-09-19. Same specificity class as the
   panel-inset and mobile-collapse regressions.
10. **"Specialties" and "Shoots" sit side by side with no explanation.** Both
    answer "what do they do"; only Shoots has a hint.
11. **`Photographers` is required, `Videographers` is not**, so a video-only
    package has a required field to zero out.
12. **Package description truncates mid-word** with no title attribute.
13. **The Cue chat column is ~280px at 1024px wide** — narrower than the quick
    actions rail beside it. Fine at 1440.
14. **Status badges clip at the card edge** on the People lists below ~900px —
    "accepted" renders as "accepte…". Pre-existing, not touched here: the row
    grid reflows to `1fr 1fr` at that breakpoint and the badge keeps its column.

## Retracted

**Service areas are not silently dropped.** I logged this as a P0 after watching
the form submit with uncommitted text in the area box. Wrong:
`place-tags-field.tsx:166` commits on `onBlur`, so the submit press commits the
tag on its way. Verified in Firestore — the profile stored
`serviceAreas: ["New York City"]`. Caught by reading the stored document
instead of trusting the screenshot.

## Unresolved

**One crew profile saved an empty trade after the box was visibly ticked.**
Marco Silva was created with the Videographer pill ticked in the screenshot
taken immediately before submit, and stored `trades: []`. A second profile made
the same way stored `["videographer"]`. **Not reproducible**: ticking the box
and then typing elsewhere keeps `getAll("trades") === ["videographer"]`, so I am
not claiming a defect. Worth a Playwright case anyway — the failure is silent,
and finding 9 makes the pill's hit area behave unlike anything else on the form.

## Left open, on purpose

**Naming the crew in the journey rail.** Finding 6's ideal was the rail's
"Crew confirmed · All 1 offered role accepted" row naming the people, so the
question is answered with no scrolling at all. `projectJourney` is a pure
module that takes a *count*, and it is shared with Today — widening its
contract to carry names is more than it is worth now the panel sits at 48% of
the page instead of 87%.

**An authenticated end-to-end path.** The one unresolved observation — a crew
profile that saved `trades: []` after the box was visibly ticked — wants a
Playwright case that ticks, submits and reads the stored value back. No
existing spec signs in: they all exercise public or preview surfaces against
`npm run start`, and a crew profile needs a real session against the emulator.
Building that harness is its own piece of work, and faking coverage for it
would be worse than saying so. The layout defect that made the pill's hit area
behave oddly (finding 9) is fixed, which removes the most plausible cause.
