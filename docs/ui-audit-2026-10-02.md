# Studio console visual audit — 2026-10-02

**Prompt:** a visual click-through of the studio workspace, looking at layout,
button position, container colours, and anything that makes the right content
hard to see or find. Conor's screenshots showed three examples:

- the job header's readiness link sitting off the button row;
- the empty "Something changed with this job" card;
- the unread badge covering the time in Messages.

**How it was walked**

- Production (studio-cue.com), signed in as FlawlessIQ, in Chrome at 1440×900.
- Pages walked: Today, the Smith Wedding job (Overview, Booking, Plan and
  Delivery tabs), Messages, Inquiries, Jobs, Calendar, Clients, Insights,
  Library, Studio settings, Cue, and Notifications.
- Every collapsed panel on the job page was opened.
- An in-page check ran on the job page. It looked for:
  - text under 11px;
  - contrast below AA;
  - buttons with no fill and no border;
  - sideways overflow.
- Computed styles were read for anything that looked wrong.
- **Not covered:** phone width. The Chrome window would not resize below
  desktop. Help & guides and the settings sub-pages were not opened.

Severity:

- **P0:** a control that can't be seen or can't be trusted.
- **P1:** misaligned or broken layout.
- **P2:** polish and consistency.

## The root causes (fix these and most findings close)

| # | Cause | Findings it explains |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Components built for a dark surface are reused on white cards.** The stage control and the interruption control were written for the old dark job hero: white-alpha borders, light-grey text, 9.5px type. The stage control got a `.job-rail-card` override (`app/job-thread.css:1101`). The interruption control never did. | 1, 7 |
| B | **There is no global disabled style for buttons.** Disabled styling exists only where a component remembered to add it (8 separate `:disabled` rules in `globals.css`). Any button without one looks fully live: full colour, pointer cursor. This is the same allowlist pattern the panel inset had. | 2 |
| C | **There is no single primary-button treatment.** We counted five "main action" colours. Components set their own gradient (`globals.css:14093`, `14872`, …). | 18 |
| D | **The muted text token is too light.** `--muted` / `--ds-muted` = `#8b929d`, which is **3.1:1** on white (AA needs 4.5). It is used for tab labels, field labels, subtitles and secondary buttons ("Ask Cue" / "Add a task" are 2.8:1). | 19, 21, plus most "hard to see" text |
| E | **Table headers and rows are separate elements with different insets.** Column headers sit 8–24px to the right of their data. | 6 |
| F | **Different panels on the job page compute "what's outstanding" separately**, so they disagree. This is data, not CSS, but it does the most damage to trust. | 3 |

## P0 — can't be seen, or can't be trusted

1. **Job page → "Something changed with this job" (right rail).**
   - **Closed:** 32px of empty space sits above a 10.5px grey summary. This is
     the `margin-top:17px` + `padding-top:15px` from the dark-hero rule, plus
     the card's own padding.
   - **Open:**
     - the explanations are **9.5px, rgb(159,171,163) on white, about 2:1**;
     - **"Put the job on hold" has no fill and no border**, so it reads as a
       stray caption, not a button;
     - "Cancel the job" is a beige blob.
   - Hold and cancel are the two controls a studio needs when a wedding moves,
     and they are close to invisible.
   - *Fix:* a `.job-rail-card .project-interruption-control` override, matching
     the stage control's. Real buttons: hold as secondary, cancel as danger.
     Text at 13px, `--ds-text-2`.

2. **Delivery tab → "Release to the couple" is disabled but looks live.**
   - Computed: `disabled=true`, opacity 1, `cursor:pointer`, full emerald
     gradient.
   - The only clue is a cream note above it saying it is not ready.
   - *Fix:* a global `:where(.button, button):disabled { opacity:.45;
     cursor:not-allowed; filter:saturate(.3) }` in `legacy-bridge.css`, using
     the zero-specificity default pattern from the panel-inset fix.

3. **The job page tells three different stories about the same job**
   (Smith Wedding). The disagreements:
   - **Header:** "9 things before the day: **Retainer paid** +8 more".
   - **Retainer:**
     - Journey rail: ✓ Retainer paid.
     - "Client needs" lane: *Retainer paid — Due Jun 16, 2026*.
     - Booking tab: Retainer · **Paid**.
   - **Contract:** Journey shows ✓ Contract signed, but Client needs shows
     *Sign the contract — Not sent yet*.
   - **Crew:**
     - Journey: ✓ Crew confirmed, "Shooting this one solo".
     - Readiness: ✓ Required crew accepted.
     - Crew card: *Nobody is booked … earlier offers have lapsed*.
     - Plan tab: Crew **Done**, but every person listed is Cancelled or
       Expired.
     - Today: *Still no second photographer* ×2.
   - **Counts:** 9 things (header) vs Readiness **3/7** vs Journey **7/14**.
   - *Fix:* one outstanding-items reader that the header link, the lanes, the
     checkpoints and the journey all read from (root cause F). This is a
     logic change, not a style change, and should be scoped on its own.

## P1 — layout and alignment

4. **Job header readiness link.**
   - It is a direct child of `<header>`, so it sits outside
     `.project-title-actions`
     ([live-project-detail.tsx:1547](components/projects/live-project-detail.tsx:1547)).
     It centres against the whole title column, lands about 16px below the
     buttons, and is pushed to the far right edge.
   - It is underlined body text, not a control.
   - "Retainer paid" reads as good news when it is a missing item.
   - *Fix:*
     - Move it into the actions row as a pill: an amber dot plus "9 before the
       day".
     - Name the first item as a task ("Retainer not paid").

5. **Messages → the unread badge covers the timestamp.**
   - `.msg-thread-badge` is `position:absolute; top:12px; right:12px`
     ([communications.css:123](app/communications.css:123)), exactly where
     `.msg-thread-when` sits. "38m ago" renders as "38m ●".
   - *Fix:* put the badge in the flex row after the time.

6. **Jobs and Inquiries tables: headers don't line up with their columns.**
   - "DATE & VENUE" starts 8px right of the dates, "STATE" 15px, "VALUE" 20px,
     "NEXT ACTION" 24px.
   - Stage pills stretch to fill the whole column, so "New" is a 180px pill.
   - *Fix:*
     - Share one grid template and one inline padding between the header and
       the rows.
     - Size pills to their content.

7. **Job page → "Move this project forward".**
   - It uses a raw `▼` glyph, while every other disclosure uses a chevron icon.
   - "CURRENT STAGE" wraps to two lines and "NEXT STAGE" doesn't, so
     **Booked** and **Planning** sit at different heights either side of the
     arrow.
   - *Fix:*
     - Use the chevron.
     - Make the labels "Now" / "Next", or put both on one line.

8. **Insights header buttons wrap their own labels.**
   - "Release / evidence" and "Export / CSV" break onto two lines inside the
     button.
   - *Fix:* `white-space:nowrap`, and move "Release evidence" (an internal
     concept) off the studio page.

9. **Insights KPI tiles.**
   - The headline numbers (11, 26%, $28,197, $10,188) are **light grey**, so
     they read as disabled.
   - A 40px green stub under each tile looks like a progress bar but encodes
     nothing.
   - In the outcomes grid, two-line labels ("AUTOMATIONS COMPLETED") push their
     number below the numbers in the neighbouring tiles.
   - *Fix:*
     - Numbers in `--ds-text`.
     - Drop the stubs.
     - Labels to one line, or `align-items:end` on the number row.

10. **Calendar event chips truncate job names to about 8 characters**
    ("Smith We…", "Alex & Sa…") inside 100px cells.
    - *Fix:* let the chip use the full cell width and wrap to two lines.

11. **Clients rows are twice as tall as they need to be.**
    - The actions (Edit or archive, Invite to portal) take a second line on
      every row.
    - The **Company** column is "—" for every wedding client.
    - "Projects 1" doesn't say, or link to, *which* job.
    - *Fix:*
      - Put the actions in-row on the right.
      - Swap Company for the job name as a link.

12. **Notifications (bell).**
    - It opens a full page, and the sidebar highlights **Today**.
    - Each row is a data dump: Due "—", Priority "normal", Blocking "No".
    - The arrow sits on its own line under the title.
    - **Complete** tasks are listed.
    - The tab title is "Action queue".
    - The bell has no unread count.
    - *Fix:* compact rows (title · job · due), hide completed items, a count on
      the bell, and correct nav state.

13. **Today → card titles don't share a left edge.**
    - Cards with an icon start their title at x=370. Cards without one ("The
      consultation call wasn't booked", "The invoice wasn't created") start at
      x=328.
    - Some cards have no job name ("Still no second videographer", "Still no
      videographer 2", one "The invoice wasn't created"), so you can't tell
      what they belong to.
    - *Fix:*
      - Always reserve the icon column.
      - Always show the job chip.

14. **Today hero.**
    - About 110px of empty dark band sits below the divider line.
    - "Open the task" is a fixed, oddly wide 360px pill.
    - *Fix:*
      - Size the hero to its content, or show the next two items under the
        divider.
      - Size the button to its content.

15. **Job page → crew card.**
    - "Staff this job" (the main action) and "Not me this time" are plain grey
      text with no button styling.
    - The green notice is indented 28px from the card's text edge.
    - *Fix:*
      - "Staff this job" as the card's primary button.
      - "Not me this time" as a quiet button.
      - The notice flush with the text.

16. **Readiness checkpoints: the visual weight is inverted.**
    - Pending rows are grey-filled, so they look disabled.
    - Done rows are white with a border, so they look active.
    - "Waive" is a bordered button on some rows and faded text on others.
    - *Fix:* pending rows white, done rows quiet. Use one Waive style.

17. **The Delivery tab shows the full release form before the wedding.**
    - The form, its fields and a live-looking Release button all show 12 days
      before the event, with a "not ready" note at the bottom.
    - *Fix:* collapse to one line ("Opens after Oct 14") until the event has
      been covered.

## P2 — colour system and consistency

18. **There are five primary-button colours.**

    | Colour | Where it's used |
    |---|---|
    | Flat emerald `#0ea372` | "New project" (top bar) |
    | Emerald gradient | "Create project", "Preview inquiry form", "Send reply", "Release to the couple", "Chase payment" (Plan tab) |
    | Forest green | "Approve & send" (Today) |
    | Near-black | "Resolve", "Find crew", "Confirm planning has started", "Open the task" |
    | Aubergine | "Resume import" (Library) |

    A user can't learn "this colour = the thing to press".
    - *Fix:* one primary token and one secondary token.

19. **Contrast: muted text is 3.1:1** (root cause D).
    - The top-bar "New project" is white on `#0ea372`, which is **3.0:1**.
    - *Fix:* darken `--ds-muted` to about `#6b7280` (4.8:1). Darken the primary
      to `#0a8a5f` or use `#0a7a55`.

20. **Status pills don't mean one thing.**
    - "Booked" is green in the job bar and blue-grey on the Confirm-booking
      card.
    - On Inquiries, "New" and "Consult" share the same amber. The one needing
      action should stand out.
    - "Portal active" is blue, which is used nowhere else.

21. **Links that look disabled.**
    - "Open proposal" (Booking tab) is grey.
    - Archive job is grey text with an icon.
    - "Staff this job" (see 15).

22. **Calendar.**
    - The "Project event" and "Booked" legend dots are both amber and can't be
      told apart.
    - **Weekends use the same grey fill as past days**, so a wedding
      photographer's Saturdays look closed.
    - "Manage availability" (header) and "Availability settings" (card) do the
      same thing.

23. **Confidence badges ("95%", "70%") on the prepared tray have no label.**
    - "Delivery message draft" is the label on a *pre-wedding* invoice notice.

## Outcome (same day)

Every finding was acted on, except one false finding and two kept by design
(listed below). Each was checked on the local emulator at 1440px.

**System fixes (root causes A–F)**

- **A.** The interruption control got the same rail override the stage control
  had, plus:
  - one chevron for both disclosures;
  - "Now → Next" on one line;
  - hold as an outlined button;
  - cancel as an outlined danger button.

  [app/job-thread.css](../app/job-thread.css)
- **B.** A global disabled style:
  `.ds-root :is(button, .button, .ds-btn, [role=button]):is(:disabled, [aria-disabled=true])`
  in [app/legacy-bridge.css](../app/legacy-bridge.css).
- **C.** One primary fill, `--ds-action-fill`. `.button-dark`,
  `.ds-btn-primary`, `.ds-action`, Today's primary, the import banner and the
  stage card all read it.
- **D.** `--ds-muted` changed from `#8b929d` (3.1:1) to `#6b7280` (4.8:1).
- **E.** Table header and row grids now use `minmax(0, …)` with a fixed 30px
  last track. The stage pill hugs its content.
- **F.** The job page now reads one set of facts:
  - the header and the job bar skip archived checkpoints;
  - the booking gate counts as evidence of the contract and the retainer
    (`bookingConfirmed` in both copies of `readinessEvidenceFromFacts`);
  - an unsent draft after a signed agreement is not "Sign the contract";
  - drafts waiting on the studio are listed in the prepared tray only.

**Also discovered while fixing**

- **The domain list's phone line-break was a grid cell on desktop.**
  `.live-domain-table span { display: grid }` out-ranked the rule that hid it.
  Every domain list (Open tasks, Documents, Invoices…) had one cell too many,
  so its arrow wrapped onto a line of its own. That was the real cause of
  finding 12's arrow.
- **`--green` was read by 14 rules and defined nowhere.** That caused the
  ink-black border on "Confirm booking". It is now mapped to the action green.
- **Success badges were teal-blue.** The emerald theme moved `--ds-forest` to
  teal-blue, so every success badge ("Booked", "Portal active") was blue.
  `.status-success` is green now.
- **Messages split one couple into two threads.** A form inquiry's
  acknowledgement, queued before the lead converted, recreated the lead thread
  after the move. Two fixes:
  - `resolveThreadScope` (functions) files a converted lead's sends on the job;
  - `groupConversations` folds threads that are already split.

**False finding**

- **14, the Today hero's empty band.** The stats and countdown have an entrance
  animation with `animation-fill-mode: backwards`. Chrome does not run it in a
  background tab, so they sat at opacity 0 during the walk. In a visible tab
  they render. Only the stretched "Open the task" button was real (fixed with
  `justify-self: start`).

**Kept by design**

- **25, the Overview header vs the compact bar on the other tabs.** The
  Overview is the job's home and keeps its full header and actions. The
  compact bar now speaks the same language: "N things before the day" in place
  of the unlabelled ring. Both bars read the same unarchived checkpoints.
- **20, Inquiries "New" and "Consult" share amber.** On that list, colour
  means whose move it is (amber is the studio's), not the stage. The row
  already says "Your move".
- **27, the two questionnaire nudges on Smith Wedding.** They answer two
  different forms. Grouping keys on the records a draft answers
  (`answeredRecords`), so they are not duplicates.
