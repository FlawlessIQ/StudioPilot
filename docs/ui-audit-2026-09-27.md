# UI audit — 2026-09-27

**Prompt:** the job page's new "Add a client" and "Edit job" buttons were laid
out badly and got worse when opened; the client list stacked its buttons; the
Team page had icon buttons with no explanation. "Find similar issues across the
platform."

**How it was walked**

- Production, signed in as FlawlessIQ, in Chrome. An in-page check ran on
  every studio page (47 routes, plus each settings section, job tab and create
  form). It opened each collapsed toggle, then looked for:
  - an icon stacked above its label;
  - native grey buttons;
  - icon-only controls with no hover text;
  - form labels laid inline with their fields;
  - sideways overflow.
- A static scan of every `className` in `app/`, `components/` and `features/`
  against every selector in `app/*.css`. Every unmatched name was then read in
  context to separate real breakage from harmless modifiers.
- Fixes checked on the local emulator at 1440px and 375px.
- Not covered: the client portal and crew workspace. They need their own
  accounts. The static scan covers their code, but no one walked them.

## The root cause, three times over

**Class names that no stylesheet defines.** A component named
`ghost-button`, `record-edit-form` or `primary-button`. Nothing styled them, so
they rendered as browser defaults:
- the icon sat on its own line above the words;
- buttons were native grey;
- each label ran into its field across the line.

This survived review because nothing compares the names components use with
the names the CSS defines. `tests/unstyled-classes.test.ts` now does.

## Findings and fixes

| # | Where | What was wrong | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Job page: Add a client, Edit job | Unstyled classes. The icon sat above the label. Opening either spilled an unstyled form into the title line, and squeezed the readiness link into a narrow column. | Small outlined buttons beside the name. Each opens a sheet with a two-column form, a heading, and Cancel / Save. |
| 2 | Clients, Crew, Vendors rows | Each disclosure (Edit or archive, Invite to portal, Share run of show) took a full-width line of its own, with the icon stacked above the words. | One actions cell per row: small outlined buttons side by side with the icon inline. An opened one takes the full width for its form and shows as active. |
| 3 | Team page: members | Clients and crew with accounts were listed as team members. Their role picker fell back to its first option, so a client showed as "Studio Admin", and changing the picker **made the client a coordinator** (the server accepted it). | The page lists studio staff only. `updateMember` refuses any membership that isn't a staff role (`MEMBER_NOT_EDITABLE`). |
| 4 | Team page: actions | Suspend was an unlabelled icon that acted on one click. Changing a role gave no word on what the role could do. Removing someone wasn't offered, though the server supports it. | Labelled Suspend / Reactivate / Remove, each asking first. Every role has a one-line summary (`features/team/role-summaries.ts`, tested against `rolePermissions`). Avatars, as on Clients. |
| 5 | Team page: copy | After inviting: "Outbound email remains gated until the sending domain is approved". The invitation email is sent. Decorative icons in each card's corner looked like buttons. "Least-privilege roles … tenant credentials". | The notice says the invitation was sent. Decorative icons removed. Plain heading. |
| 6 | Everywhere: icon-only controls | 30+ icon buttons (notifications, calendar arrows, the Jobs table's open arrows, remove-a-question, remove-a-time-range, the sheet close button, …) had a screen-reader name but no hover text. | `IconButtonTitles` at the app root turns any icon-only control's `aria-label` into its tooltip, including controls added later. |
| 7 | Job tabs: Booking / Plan / Delivery | Plan and Delivery put a page title above the job bar and Booking didn't, so the tab bar jumped between tabs. | Opened for a job, the job bar is the heading on all three (tab bar at the same 112px). |
| 8 | Settings → Crew offers | Rendered with no card or heading: two bare checkboxes, and a label inline with its field. | A card with a heading, like every other section. |
| 9 | Delivery closeout: "Mark as done" | Native grey button on desktop. Its only rule was inside a phone media query. | `button button-quiet button-sm`. |
| 10 | Delivery closeout: the attest form | Unstyled. "How do you know?", the field and the caveat ran together on one line. | Stacked form rules. |
| 11 | Proposal: Discount field | Label, field and hint ran inline. | Reuses `.proposal-field`. |
| 12 | Email branding: Remove logo | Unstyled, full-width native button. | `button button-ghost`. |
| 13 | Help: section headings | `.section-label` doesn't exist, so the headings rendered as body text. | `eyebrow`. |
| 14 | Crew: availability edit / remove | Native grey 44px boxes. | Bordered like the rest of the crew workspace. |

## Left as they are

- The job header's "N things before the day" link wraps onto two lines
  when the job name is long. It's readable, and it no longer collapses
  now that the forms open in a sheet.
- The Team page can't edit a member's name. The name comes from their own
  account, which they set when they join.
