# Vendor journeys: DJ, makeup, hair

Brainstorm, 2026-10-08 (Conor: "take on the personas of dj, make up and hair
and brainstorm on the 3 journeys … reuse the existing features so that it is
the same as photography but labelled differently as a journey"). Not built.

**Decided since (Conor, 2026-10-08):** hair and makeup are **separate
journeys** (sharing a beauty core); these trades are **$75/month**; no
overtime yet. The execution plan is `docs/vendor-journeys-plan.md`.
Sources are listed at the end; figures marked *(snippet)* came from search
summaries of pages that blocked reading.

## The short version

- **The spine is the same.** All three trades run inquiry → reply → (call) →
  proposal → agreement → retainer → planning form → plan of the day → final
  call → team → insurance → balance → day before → the day → review. Every
  one of those is a StudioCue step today.
- **What's different is small and specific:**
  - Nobody delivers anything afterwards. No gallery, album or editing, so the
    journey ends at the review.
  - The DJ's planning form is a music and announcements planner, and its plan
    of the day is an MC script.
  - Hair and makeup price per person. They have a paid trial, and their plan of
    the day is a getting-ready chair schedule worked back from the
    photographer's ready-by time.
- **Hair and makeup are one journey.** Most teams sell both, quote "hair +
  makeup" or "makeup only", and run the chairs together. Recommendation: one
  **Hair & makeup** trade where the studio says which services it offers.
  That gives two new journeys, **DJ** and **Hair & makeup**, alongside
  **Photography**.

## What's reused vs. what's new

**Reused with a new label** (most of it):

| Exists today | DJ | Hair & makeup |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry form + event types | DJ fields (hours, ceremony?) | Ready-by, access, headcount by role |
| Cue first reply, follow-ups, LOST | same | same |
| Consultation booking link | "Vibe call" | **Trial** (a booking with `purpose: "trial"`, like the final details call's `purpose`) |
| Packages + add-ons with quantity | Packages + add-ons | Per-person services are add-ons with a quantity |
| Proposal, native e-sign, Schedule A | Venue, load-in, power, sound | Address, ready-by, minimum, prep rules |
| Retainer, final balance, payment plans | same | + "due on the day" |
| Recommended forms, prefill, autosave, reminders | Music & moments planner | Party list |
| Details lock + couple sign-off | Planner lock 1–2 weeks out | Headcount lock ~30 days out |
| Booking amendments | — | Adding people after the lock |
| Simple timeline / run of show + PDF | MC script | Getting-ready schedule |
| Final details call | 1 week out | Optional |
| Crew, first call, offers, monthly roundup | DJs | Assistant artists |
| COI to venue | same | Rarely |
| Day-before checklist, event reminder | Load-in checklist | Kit checklist, prep guide |
| Review request, thank-you | same | same |
| Vendor run-of-show share | With the photographer and planner | Gets the photographer's ready-by time |

**New** (the build list):

1. **Studio trade.** A `tenants.trade` field (photographer | dj | beauty), with
   vocabulary and a profile per trade, alongside job kind. Signup asks for it,
   and starter forms, packages, workflows and moments are chosen by trade.
2. **Journey with no delivery step.** Skip delivery and album, and go from
   EVENT_COMPLETE straight to REVIEW_REQUESTED. Status labels per trade
   ("Played", "Done"). Turn off gallery, album and expiry emails.
3. **DJ: Music & moments planner** (a recommended form) and **MC script
   fields** on the run of show (song, announcement and pronunciation per
   moment).
4. **DJ: overtime.** Add hours on the night, then bill them (a one-tap extra
   invoice).
5. **H&M: per-person pricing.** Add-ons get a unit label ("per person") and a
   minimum (people or dollars), and the quote shows people × price.
6. **H&M: trial.** A booking purpose plus a trial fee (charged, or credited
   against the retainer), with trial notes and photos kept on the job.
7. **H&M: party list.** A repeating "one row per person" form field. Check
   whether forms already support repeating rows.
8. **H&M: chair schedule generator.** Ready-by time, minutes per service per
   person and number of artists produce slots, with the bride in the middle
   and the number of artists needed.
9. **H&M: balance due on the day** as a payment option, and tip lines.
10. **Hair: extensions reminder** (order 6–8 weeks out, rental return) as a
    Today task, plus a **prep guide** email.

## Suggested order

1. **Trade foundation** (#1, #2), with DJ and H&M vocabularies.
2. **DJ** (#3, #4). The smallest change, and Gabe works with DJs.
3. **Hair & makeup** (#5–#10). The biggest change.
4. **Cross-vendor links.** The photographer's timeline sets the H&M ready-by
   time; DJs share the run of show with the photographer. Then resume
   referrals (photographers ↔ DJs ↔ H&M).
