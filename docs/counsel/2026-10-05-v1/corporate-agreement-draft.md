# Corporate event agreement — starter draft for counsel

**Status: draft for counsel review. Not offered in the product.** Once
approved, it would be offered (like the wedding starter in
`agreement-text.md`) to a studio that has no corporate agreement of its own.
The studio adopts and edits it; StudioCue is not a party.

Why a separate starter: GR Productions confirmed corporate work needs a
signed agreement (family and sports sessions do not), and corporate clients
ask different questions from couples — commercial usage rights, invoicing on
terms, insurance, confidentiality and releases for employees on camera.

Fields in `{{…}}` are filled from the job (the same merge fields as every
StudioCue agreement). Bracketed text marks a choice the studio makes.

---

```text
This Photography Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on {{contract.date}} between {{studio.legal_name}}, of {{studio.address}} (the "Studio"), and {{client.names}} (the "Client") for {{event.type}} coverage of {{event.name}} on {{event.date}} at {{event.venue}} (the "Event").

## 1. Services
The Studio will provide the {{package.name}}: {{package.coverage}}. Deliverables:
{{package.deliverables}}
Coverage beyond the hours above, additional locations, or work requested after the Event (for example extra retouching) is quoted and agreed in writing before it is done.

## 2. Client responsibilities
The Client will (a) give the Studio a schedule, a shot list and a single on-site contact at least [7] days before the Event; (b) arrange access, permits, parking and any venue approvals; (c) tell the Studio of any restrictions on photography, including people, areas or materials that must not be photographed; and (d) obtain any consents its employees, guests or speakers need to give before being photographed, unless the parties agree in writing that the Studio will collect releases.

## 3. Fees and payment
The total fee is {{price.total}}, payable as follows:
{{payment.schedule}}
Invoices are due [within 30 days of the invoice date]. Overdue amounts accrue interest at [1.5% per month] or the highest rate allowed by law, whichever is lower, and the Studio may withhold delivery of final images while an undisputed amount is overdue. The Client pays any reasonable costs of collecting overdue amounts. Fees exclude sales tax, which is added where it applies. Purchase order numbers or vendor registration the Client needs must be supplied before the Event; their absence does not delay payment.

## 4. Changes, postponement and cancellation
A change to the date, hours or scope must be agreed in writing; fees are adjusted to match. If the Client cancels [more than 30] days before the Event, the retainer is retained and nothing further is owed; [within 30 days], [50%] of the total fee is owed; [within 7 days], the full fee is owed. A postponement to a date the Studio can cover, requested at least [14] days ahead, is not a cancellation; the retainer moves to the new date once. If the Studio cannot perform because of illness, injury or an emergency, it will make reasonable efforts to provide a qualified replacement photographer; if it cannot, it refunds all amounts paid, which is the Client's sole remedy.

## 5. Delivery
Edited images are delivered by online gallery within [10 business days] of the Event, or as stated in the deliverables. The Studio selects and edits images in its professional judgment and is not obliged to deliver unedited files. The Studio keeps the delivered images for at least [90 days] after delivery; the Client is responsible for downloading and keeping its own copies.

## 6. Ownership and licence
The Studio owns the copyright in all images. On payment in full, the Studio grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide licence to use, reproduce and display the delivered images for the Client's own [internal and external marketing, communications, social media, websites and press] purposes. The licence does not include (a) resale, sublicensing or transfer of the images to third parties other than the Client's agencies working on its behalf; (b) use in paid advertising placements beyond [the Client's own channels]; or (c) use by sponsors, vendors or speakers in their own promotion — each of which needs a separate written licence. The Client will credit the Studio where credits are customarily given. Until payment in full, no licence is granted.

## 7. Releases and recognisable people
Unless the parties agree in writing that the Studio collects model releases, the Client is responsible for obtaining any consent or release needed for its intended use of images showing recognisable people, and for complying with its own policies on photographing employees, guests and minors. The Studio may decline to photograph any person who asks not to be photographed.

## 8. Studio's portfolio use
The Studio may use a limited selection of images in its portfolio, website and social media [unless the Client asks in writing, before the Event, that it does not]. The Studio will not use images that show confidential information, and will remove a portfolio image on the Client's reasonable request.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep confidential any non-public information it receives from the other in connection with the Event, use it only to perform this Agreement, and protect it with reasonable care. This does not cover information that is public, already known to the recipient, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 10. Insurance
The Studio carries general liability insurance of at least [$1,000,000 per occurrence] and will provide a certificate of insurance naming the Client or venue as additional insured on request, at least [5] business days before the Event.

## 11. Liability
The Studio is not responsible for images it could not take because of venue restrictions, the Client's schedule, weather, lighting, or the conduct of people at the Event. The Studio's total liability arising out of this Agreement is limited to the fees paid under it. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or lost profits, except for a breach of Section 9 or a party's indemnity obligations. Each party indemnifies the other against third-party claims arising from its own negligence or wilful misconduct, and the Client indemnifies the Studio against claims arising from the Client's use of images beyond the licence in Section 6 or from the Client's failure to obtain consents under Section 7.

## 12. General
This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Event and replaces any earlier proposal or discussion, except that the Client's accepted proposal is incorporated for the packages and prices it lists. It may be changed only in writing signed by both parties. It is governed by the laws of [the state where the Studio is based]. If any part is unenforceable, the rest stands. Neither party may assign it without the other's consent, except the Studio may engage qualified associate photographers. Notices go to the email addresses on file. The parties may sign electronically, and in counterparts.

{{event.details}}
```

## Questions for counsel

1. Is the licence scope in §6 a sensible default for corporate clients, and
   should "paid advertising" be carved out by default or left to the studio?
2. §7 puts releases on the client by default. Is that the right default, and
   does any state's law (e.g. biometric or employee-privacy statutes) need a
   mention?
3. Late-payment interest and collection costs (§3): enforceable as drafted
   across US states, or should the studio pick a rate per state?
4. Mutual indemnities (§11): appropriate for a small studio, or should the
   studio's indemnity be capped with the liability cap?
5. Schedule A (`{{event.details}}`) carries the event details; for corporate
   jobs there is no "lock" date (job-kinds: only weddings lock). Does the
   agreement need any wording about late schedule changes beyond §4?
